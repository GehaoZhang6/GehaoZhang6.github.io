Research Highlights

Grounded Action Model: 3D Grounding as a Foundation for Robotics [Sep 2026]

My research focuses on embodied AI and 3D vision, with the aim of finding general representations for robots. Towards this goal, I am currently interested in:

  • Foundation models for embodied AI. What a robot policy should inherit from pretrained models, and how far a frozen backbone can carry it.
  • 3D vision for robotics. Object-centric, metric scene understanding that tells a policy which objects matter and where they are.
